# Multi-Dimensional Scoring Engine & Money Management (MM)

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Weighting Framework
Decision signals are generated by analyzing three distinct, decoupled dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Primary Indicators & Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Fundamental** | **20%** | P/E (<18), P/BV (<2.0), ROE (>10%), D/E (<1.5x), Net Margin (>5%), Dividend Yield (>3%). |
| **Technical** | **40%** | Trend (Price > EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200), Momentum (RSI 14 between 50-65), MACD (Line > Signal & Hist > 0). |
| **Volume (VSA)** | **40%** | Volume Ratio vs 20-day SMA (>1.4x), Price-Volume expansion (Bullish close in upper 70% of bar), Volume EMA5 > EMA20. |

$$\text{Raw Score} = (\text{Fund} \times 0.20) + (\text{Tech} \times 0.40) + (\text{Vol} \times 0.40)$$
$$\text{Final Score} = \text{Raw Score} \times \text{Regime Multiplier (0.7 - 1.0)}$$

## 2. Signal Thresholds & Execution Actions
- **>= 80% (STRONG_BUY):** Auto-execute Call DW + Trailing Stop armed.
- **70% - 79% (BUY):** Auto-execute Call DW with OCO Limit (SL & TP).
- **50% - 69% (HOLD / WATCH):** Queue interactive alert (Telegram/Webhook) for manual discretion.
- **< 50% (AVOID):** Reject trade to protect capital from Time Decay.

## 3. Strict Money Management (1% Max Risk Rule)
```python
risk_budget = capital * 0.01  # e.g. 50,000 * 0.01 = 500 THB
risk_per_share = (dw_entry - dw_sl) + 0.01  # include 1-tick slippage buffer
raw_shares = risk_budget / risk_per_share
# Round down to SET Board Lot (multiples of 100 shares)
recommended_volume = int(raw_shares // 100) * 100

# Portfolio Heat Guard: Single position outlay must not exceed 25% of total capital
if (recommended_volume * dw_entry) > (capital * 0.25):
    recommended_volume = int(((capital * 0.25) / dw_entry) // 100) * 100
```

## 4. PostgreSQL Telemetry Tables (`finance_db`)
- `fundamental_evaluations`: Stores valuation ratios, margin, and fundamental scores.
- `technical_evaluations`: Stores EMAs, RSI, MACD, and price support/resistance.
- `volume_evaluations`: Stores volume ratios and VSA patterns.
- `composite_signals`: Stores aggregated multi-dim scores, signal type, and DW targets.
- `dw_catalog`: Tracks active DW symbols, gearing, and expiry dates.
