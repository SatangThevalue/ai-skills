# Content Telemetry & Optimization Triage Matrix

Framework for evaluating post-publish social media video performance (Facebook Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) and diagnosing structural fixes for subsequent iterations.

---

## 1. Key Metrics & Mathematical Formulas

| Metric | Formula | Benchmark | Algorithmic Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Hook Retention Rate** | $\frac{\text{3-Second Views}}{\text{Total Impressions}} \times 100$ | **> 65%** | Passing this threshold promotes the video beyond existing followers into Discovery / For You feeds. |
| **Completion Rate (VTR)** | $\frac{\text{Completed Views}}{\text{Total Video Starts}} \times 100$ | **> 35%** (20–30s)<br>**> 60%** (7–11s) | Primary signal of pacing efficiency and viewer satisfaction. |
| **Save Rate (High-Value)** | $\frac{\text{Saves}}{\text{Total Reach}} \times 100$ | **> 3.0%** | In Meta algorithms, 1 Save equals ~5 Likes in distribution weight for educational/utility content. |
| **Share Velocity** | $\frac{\text{Shares}}{\text{Total Reach}} \times 100$ | **> 1.5%** | Multiplier for viral reach; drives subtle venting or peer recommendation. |
| **Total Engagement Ratio**| $\frac{\text{Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves}}{\text{Reach}} \times 100$ | **> 8.0%** | Measures audience resonance and community stickiness. |

## 2. Iteration Triage Matrix

| Observed Diagnostic | Root Cause | Structural Remedy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Hook Rate (< 50%)** | 0–3s opening is too slow, generic greeting, or low visual contrast. | Open with the punchline immediately. Use gold/yellow highlight on key negative emotion or counter-intuitive metric. Drop greetings. |
| **Mid-Clip Drop-off (4–10s)** | Pacing lag, wall of text without motion, or monotonic audio. | Insert pattern interrupt: slide in checklist card, change background angle, or speed up narration TTS by 5–10%. |
| **High Completion, Low Saves (< 1%)** | Entertaining but lacks utility or reference value. | Add numbered takeaway cards (01–04), specific command names, or actionable steps that viewers need to save for later. |
| **High Completion, Low Shares (< 0.5%)** | Content is personal or lacks identity expression. | Align with "Subtle Venting" or "Belonging": frame copy as universal human truth that viewers share to signal their values or current emotional state. |
| **High Performance Across All Metrics** | Winner artifact. | Extract visual layout and script structure into skill templates immediately. Spin off into a multi-part series. |
