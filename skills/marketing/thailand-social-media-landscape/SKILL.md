# Thailand Social Media Landscape & User Behavior

This skill provides a comprehensive overview of how Thai people use social media, based on aggregate data from digital reports (e.g., We Are Social, ETDA, WISESIGHT). It serves as a strategic guide for digital marketing, content creation, and platform selection in Thailand.

## 1. Overall Market Snapshot
- **Internet Penetration:** ~85%+ (Over 60 million internet users).
- **Social Media Penetration:** ~75%+ (Over 50 million active social media users).
- **Daily Time Spent:** Thais spend an average of **2.5 to 3 hours per day** on social media, making it one of the most highly engaged populations globally.
- **Mobile-First:** Over 95% of social media access in Thailand is via smartphones.

## 2. Platform Breakdown & Strategies

### A. LINE (The "National App")
- **User Base:** ~54+ Million active users.
- **Demographics:** All ages (Gen Z to Baby Boomers).
- **Primary Use:** Personal chatting, Work communication, News (LINE TODAY), and interacting with businesses.
- **Business Strategy:** **LINE Official Account (LINE OA)** is mandatory for CRM, customer service, and retargeting. Thais expect to chat with a brand before buying (Conversational Commerce).

### B. Facebook (The "Mass Community")
- **User Base:** ~48-50 Million active users.
- **Demographics:** Heavy usage among Millennials, Gen X, and Boomers. Gen Z uses it mainly for specific Groups.
- **Primary Use:** News consumption, Community Groups (very strong in Thailand), Live selling, and interacting with brands.
- **Business Strategy:** Essential for brand awareness and mass reach. Focus on community building via Facebook Groups and utilizing Facebook Live for commerce.

### C. TikTok (The "Shoppertainment" Giant)
- **User Base:** ~40+ Million users (Explosive growth).
- **Demographics:** Dominated by Gen Z and Millennials, but rapidly adopted by Gen X.
- **Primary Use:** Entertainment, trend discovery, and **Shopping**. 
- **Business Strategy:** **TikTok Shop** is revolutionizing Thai e-commerce. Content must be entertaining, raw, and authentic (not overly produced). Affiliate marketing with creators is highly effective.

### D. X / Twitter (The "Real-time Fandom")
- **User Base:** ~14-15 Million active users.
- **Demographics:** Gen Z and Millennials.
- **Primary Use:** Real-time news, trending topics, and **Fandoms** (K-Pop, T-Pop, BL/Y-Series).
- **Business Strategy:** Brands must be fast, witty, and conversational. Excellent for trend-jacking and engaging with intense fandom communities. Use hashtags strategically.

### E. Instagram (The "Visual Lifestyle")
- **User Base:** ~17-18 Million users.
- **Demographics:** Skews female, Gen Z and Millennials, heavily concentrated in Bangkok and urban areas.
- **Primary Use:** Lifestyle, fashion, food, travel, and following influencers.
- **Business Strategy:** High-quality aesthetics (IG Grid), heavy use of IG Stories for daily engagement, and IG Reels for organic reach.

### F. YouTube (The "Long-form & Music Hub")
- **User Base:** ~43+ Million users.
- **Primary Use:** Music listening (Luk Thung, T-Pop), Podcasts (highly popular in TH right now), and educational/lifestyle vlogs.
- **Business Strategy:** Pre-roll ads, sponsoring YouTubers, and creating high-value long-form content or entertaining commercials (Thai sad/funny commercials).

## 3. Key Thai Behavioral Trends

1. **Conversational Commerce (Chat & Shop):** Thais are unique in their preference to chat with a human (via LINE OA or FB Messenger) before making a purchase, to ask questions, request more photos, or ask for discounts, even if the website is fully automated.
2. **Social Search:** Gen Z in Thailand is increasingly using **TikTok and X** as search engines instead of Google. (e.g., searching "คาเฟ่เชียงใหม่" (Chiang Mai cafes) on TikTok for visual video reviews).
3. **The Power of Influencers (KOLs/KOCs):** Thais heavily trust peer reviews. Nano and Micro-influencers often yield better ROI for conversions than massive celebrities, who are mainly used for brand awareness.
4. **Live Commerce:** Selling via Live streams (TikTok, Shopee, Facebook) with energetic hosts (แม่ค้าออนไลน์) using flash sales and limited-time codes is a massive revenue driver.
5. **Mute Viewing:** A large percentage of Thais watch short-form videos (Reels/TikTok) with the sound off while commuting or at work. **Always use Thai subtitles/captions**.

## 4. Agent Instructions (How to apply this skill)
- When asked to create a marketing plan for Thailand, **ALWAYS include LINE OA** for retention/CRM.
- For e-commerce/retail clients, strongly recommend **TikTok Shop** and **Live Streaming**.
- Match the platform to the target audience: e.g., Do not recommend X (Twitter) for targeting senior citizens; recommend Facebook or LINE.
- When generating video content ideas, explicitly remind the user to include **large, readable Thai captions**.
